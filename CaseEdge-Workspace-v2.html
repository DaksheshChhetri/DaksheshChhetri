IV. Injuries and treatment

Ms. Alvarez was taken by ambulance from the scene to Riverside Community Hospital, where she reported neck and low back pain rated 7/10 with tingling down her left leg . A CT of the cervical spine ruled out fracture, and she was discharged with cervical and lumbar strain .

Four days later, orthopedic surgeon Dr. James Rhee found a positive straight-leg raise at 40 degrees on the left and ordered MRI studies . The April 2 lumbar MRI showed a 5 mm posterior disc protrusion at L4-L5 pressing on the left L5 nerve root . This is an objective structural injury, consistent with a high-energy rear impact.

Ms. Alvarez completed 36 sessions of physical therapy and two transforaminal epidural steroid injections . Her care paused between May 2 and June 12 while she arranged childcare for her two children, a reason her therapist recorded at the time .Proposed · needs review When she returned, her leg symptoms had not resolved .

On August 20, after five months of failed conservative care, Dr. Rhee recommended a left L4-L5 microdiscectomy at an estimated cost of $98,000 .
